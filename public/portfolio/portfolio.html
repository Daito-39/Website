3D Title Screen

Aim for this example is a combination of 2D UI elements with a 3D environment. Hovering the three choices cause animations to play related to each of the functions of the choices, as well as shifting the camera to highlight that section of the area. The capsule represents the player character, the donut represents a gear, and the double doors represent the exit. The project is built in such a way that the UI elements can be shared across multiple 3d environments, and the environments can be switched out to represent different points of the game (The inspiration for this choice is the title screen of Portal 2).
