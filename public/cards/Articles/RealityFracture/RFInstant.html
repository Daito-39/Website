Reality Fracture Instant Speed Disruption

27/9/26
This is a thing I've wanted to set myself to start doing for every set. Partially for myself, although I'm currently able to look at the set quite thoroughly to prepare myself for limited environments (although even I missed one conditional counterspell at my last prerelease), but also as a way for me to implement an easy interface for people to see the interaction the opponent could be holding mana open for. This page focuses on the cards that can interfere with you, so not included is things like the basic landcyclers.

To use this page, look at the open mana the opponent has, and click on the buttons below that they have available. everything above 4 mana is listed under 4.

For example, if your opponent has 2 plains and a swamp open, you click on White, Black and 3, and you'll get a list of the possible cards they might be holding mana open for.
