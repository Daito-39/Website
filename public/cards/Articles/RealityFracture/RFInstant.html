Reality Fracture Instant Speed Disruption

27/9/26
This is a thing I've wanted to set myself to start doing for every set. Partially for myself, although I'm currently able to look at the set quite thoroughly to prepare myself for limited environments (although even I missed one conditional counterspell at my last prerelease), but also as a way for me to implement an easy interface forpeople to see the interaction the opponent could be holding mana open for. This page focuses on the cards that can interfere with you, so not included is things like the basic landcyclers.

To use this page, look at the open mana the opponent has, and click on the buttons below that they don't have available.

For example, if your opponent has 2 plains and a swamp open, you click on Blue, Red, Green and Four or more, and you'll be left with the possibilities they have left.

I apologise for how rushed the layout is, I plan on working to improve it over the next few days. Also, I have yet to implement functionality for filtering multicolor cards by color, so for the time being they're only filtered depending on the mana value. For a quick reset you can just refresh the page.
